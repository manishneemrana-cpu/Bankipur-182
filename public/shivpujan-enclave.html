Specifications

What's built into every home

Structure
RCC frame structure; external and internal half brick work.
Doors
32mm flush door at the main entrance; 30mm ISI-mark doors elsewhere, primed and painted with stainless steel fittings.
Windows
Aluminium sliding windows throughout.
Flooring
ISI-mark 2'×2' vitrified tiles with 4" skirting; ceramic tiles in toilets; granite on the staircase.
Kitchen
Granite-polished working platform, stainless steel sink, glazed-tile dado 2' above the platform.
Toilets
Glazed tile dado up to 7', white vitreous sanitaryware, hot & cold mixer unit, geyser point provisioned.
Plumbing
Concealed PVC supply, waste and vent piping, ISI mark or equivalent.
Plaster & Paint
Smooth putty finish inside; weather-coat paint on all external wall surfaces.
Borewell
Deep tubewell with submersible pump for round-the-clock water supply.
Electrical
Concealed copper wiring, ISI mark, with adequate points in every room.
Overhead Tank
Overhead water tank of adequate capacity for the block.
Lift
One lift, standard make, serving all floors.
Common amenities

Everyday conveniences, built in

EV Charging Point
A dedicated point for residents' electric vehicles.
24-hour Power Backup
Keeps lifts, lobbies and common lighting running through outages.
Round-the-clock Water Supply
A deep borewell with submersible pump feeds the overhead tank continuously.
Convenient Elevator
One lift, standard make, serving every floor of the block.
Round-the-clock Security
A guard on duty at the gate at all hours.
Fire Fighting, per Norm
Extinguishers and safety equipment installed to code.
Car Parking
Dedicated parking space within the campus.
CCTV Surveillance, Campus-wide
Cameras covering common areas and the entrance, round the clock.
